What Do You Really Make? 🤔

Your salary is fibs! Enter what you're paid and this page will knock it down by taxes (federal plus VA state (because that's where I am)), payroll deductions, and the cost of getting to work, including the time you spend driving there, which is unpaid work in all but name. A standard 40-hour week (2,080 hours a year) is assumed. This country isn't advanced enough for a 32-hour workweek!

Estimates only: 2026 federal brackets and standard deduction, 2026 Social Security wage base, and Virginia's current brackets and standard deduction. Assumes this is your only income, no itemizing, no credits, and no local income tax (Virginia has none). Health premiums are treated as pre-tax (Section 125); 401(k) contributions reduce income tax but not FICA. "Hourly" columns divide by 2,080 paid hours. The "actually net" hourly rate in the summary divides real take-home by paid hours (plus any overtime) plus commuting hours. Overtime is assumed to be paid on all hours past 40 at your multiplier, bonuses are taxed as ordinary income, and the 401(k) match is shown separately because it isn't spendable cash.
